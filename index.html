📍 Nearby

Share your location - only with people you choose

Nearby is consent-based. You choose exactly who is in your group, and you can stop sharing in one tap. No hidden tracking, ever.

  • Your live location is shared only with members of a group you join.
  • Everyone in a group can see everyone else - sharing is mutual and transparent.
  • You can pause sharing or leave at any time.
  • Nothing is shared until you tap Start sharing below.

By continuing you agree this app is used only with the informed consent of everyone whose location is shared. Tracking a person without their knowledge is prohibited.
